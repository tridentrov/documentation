# Companion Computers for Sub


Underwater ROVs often employ a small companion computer, in addition to the [ArduPilot autopilot](/docs/sub/common-autopilots) to manage video cameras and sophisticated peripherals like SideScan Sonar as an adjunct to ArduPilot's basic vehicle control and mission execution functions. Companion computers communicate with the autopilot over a fast link, often a UART, using MAVLink messages.

ArduPilot compatible companion computers are listed [HERE](/docs/sub/companion-computers).

[Blue Robotics](https://bluerobotics.com/) offers a highly functional, open source, companion computer firmware system that runs on a [Raspberry Pi 3, 4, or 5](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-3-model-b/) for vehicles called [BlueOS](https://blueos.cloud/docs/latest/usage/overview/).
