## Past Conferences


![dev-conference-map-2024](/docs/dev/images/dev-conference-map-2024.png)
The 2024 ArduPilot developer conference was held in [Kaga, Ishikawa Japan](https://visitkaga.jp/) from Oct 25th to 27th. As with previous years the conference was a mix of presentations, live demonstrations and lots of discussion including what we should do next
